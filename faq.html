Admissions

What are the eligibility criteria for undergraduate courses? add
Eligibility depends on the specific course. Generally, students must have passed the 10+2 examination from a recognized board with minimum required percentage marks in relevant subjects. Detailed criteria are published in the admission brochure each year.
How can I apply for admission? add
The admission process is entirely online. Interested candidates should visit the admission portal via the college website during the notified admission period, fill out the form, upload necessary documents, and pay the application fee.
When is the last date to apply? add
The last date to apply is announced at the beginning of the admission cycle (usually in June/July). Please regularly check the Admissions page and Notice Board for exact deadlines.
What documents are required during admission? add
Typically required documents include Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, admit cards, recent passport-size photograph, signature, caste certificate (if applicable), and migration certificate (for students from other boards).
What is the fee structure? add
The fee structure varies by course and is subject to change. A detailed fee breakdown is available in the admission brochure and on the designated Fees portal.

Academic

What is the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)? add
CBCS offers students a choice to select courses from prescribed core, elective, or skill-based courses. It provides a 'cafeteria' type approach where students can take courses of their choice and learn at their own pace.
What is the course structure under NEP 2020? add
Following NEP 2020, UG programs are 4-year multidisciplinary courses with multiple entry and exit options, emphasizing holistic learning, skill development, and research.
What is the mandatory attendance requirement? add
Students are generally required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in both theory and practical classes to be eligible to appear for university examinations.
Where can I find the syllabus for my course? add
The detailed syllabus for all courses can be found under the Academics section of our website or directly from the respective department pages.

Examinations

Where can I check the exam schedule? add
Exam schedules are published on the college Notice Board and the Examination section of the website. Additionally, university exam dates are notified via the University of Gour Banga portal.
How are internal marks calculated? add
Internal marks are calculated based on continuous assessments which include internal tests, assignments, project work, and class attendance as per university guidelines.
When and how do I get my final marksheet? add
Mark sheets are distributed from the college office after the declaration of university results. Notifications regarding the distribution schedule will be posted on the college website.
