College History

80 Years of Educational Excellence

Founded in 1944, Malda College is one of the premier higher educational institutions in North Bengal. Situated on Rabindra Avenue in the historic city of Malda, West Bengal, the college has a glorious legacy of imparting quality education.

Initially affiliated to the University of Calcutta, and later to the University of North Bengal, the college is now affiliated to the University of Gour Banga. Established by the Government of West Bengal, Malda College is recognized by the UGC and is accredited with a NAAC B+ grade (2nd Cycle).

Over the decades, the college has evolved into a vibrant academic community, catering to thousands of students and offering a wide array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Arts, Science, and Commerce disciplines.
