Internal Complaint Cell (ICC)

ICC is constituted under the POSH Act 2013. It ensures a safe, dignified workplace by preventing, prohibiting, and redressing sexual harassment.

Objectives

What We Do

shield

Prevention

Prevent sexual harassment through awareness and strict policy enforcement.

block

Prohibition

Prohibit all forms of sexual harassment at the workplace.

gavel

Redressal

Provide timely, fair redressal of all complaints received.

lock

Confidentiality

Maintain strict confidentiality of all complaints and proceedings.
