Staff Guidelines & Rules

The Code of Conduct outlines the expected standards of behavior for all teaching and non-teaching staff at Malda College, ensuring a professional, respectful, and productive academic environment.

  1. Professional Integrity and Conduct Staff members must maintain the highest standards of professional ethics. They should perform their duties honestly, impartially, and with dedication, avoiding any action that brings disrepute to the institution.
  2. Punctuality and Attendance Strict adherence to working hours is mandatory. Prior permission or sanctioned leave must be obtained before any absence from duty. Biometric attendance should be recorded daily as per college norms.
  3. Academic Responsibility (For Teaching Staff) Teachers must complete the syllabus within the stipulated time, evaluate internal assessments fairly, and remain available to students for academic mentoring and guidance.
  4. Mutual Respect and Anti-Discrimination Staff must treat colleagues, students, and visitors with respect and dignity. No discrimination based on caste, creed, religion, gender, or physical ability will be tolerated.
  5. Use of College Resources College property, equipment, and resources must be used strictly for official and academic purposes with care and responsibility to prevent damage or wastage.
  6. Dress Code and Appearance All staff members are expected to dress decently and professionally while on campus, reflecting the dignity of an educational institution.
  7. Confidentiality Staff handling sensitive information (like examination papers, personal records, financial data) must maintain strict confidentiality and not disclose it to unauthorized persons.
