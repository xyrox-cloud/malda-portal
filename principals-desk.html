Welcome to Malda College

It is my distinct privilege and immense pleasure to welcome you to Malda College, a premier institution of higher education in North Bengal. Established in 1944, our college has continuously strived to be a beacon of knowledge and empowerment for generations of students.

As we navigate the complexities of the 21st century, the role of education has transcended traditional boundaries. We are not merely focused on academic excellence, but on holistic development. Our dedicated faculty members are committed to providing a conducive environment that fosters critical thinking, creativity, and ethical values.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. At Malda College, we are dedicated to forging this weapon with care, discipline, and a vision for a brighter tomorrow."

I am proud to mention that our recent re-accreditation by NAAC with a B+ grade (2nd Cycle) is a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality education. We are continuously upgrading our infrastructure, introducing new add-on courses, and emphasizing research and extension activities.

To our dear students: your time here is a crucial stepping stone. Embrace the opportunities, participate actively in co-curricular and extracurricular activities, and utilize the resources available to you. Let us work together to uphold the glorious legacy of Malda College and scale new heights of success.

Warm regards,
